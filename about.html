About Semai Events Decor

Our story

Creating beautiful celebrations, one detail at a time.

Semai Events Decor is an Ottawa-based event decor and styling business creating beautiful, personalized setups for life's special moments. From birthdays and bridal showers to baby showers, engagements, and other celebrations, we bring your ideas together through thoughtful styling, beautiful decor, and attention to detail.

We believe your celebration should feel like you. Whether you already have a vision in mind or need help bringing your ideas together, we work with you to create a setup that reflects your style, occasion, and personality.

As a growing local business, we care about the experience as much as the final look. We take the time to understand what you're celebrating and the feeling you want your event to have, so every detail comes together beautifully. Based in Ottawa, Ontario, Semai Events Decor proudly serves Ottawa and surrounding areas.

Let us help you turn your special occasion into a beautiful celebration.
