About Semai Events & Decor

Our story

A growing Ottawa studio for considered, personal celebrations.

Semai Events & Decor is an Ottawa-based event decor and styling business dedicated to creating beautiful, thoughtfully designed celebrations. From intimate birthday celebrations and bridal showers to baby showers, engagement celebrations, weddings, and special events, we help bring your vision to life through elegant decor, creative styling, and carefully selected details.

We believe that every celebration should feel personal and special. Whether you have a clear vision or need help bringing your ideas together, we work closely with you to create a setup that reflects your style, occasion, and personality.

As a growing local business, we take pride in providing a personalized experience and paying close attention to the little details that make an event memorable. Based in Ottawa, Ontario, Semai Events & Decor proudly serves Ottawa and surrounding areas.

Let us help you turn your special occasion into a beautiful celebration.
