Welcome

Your celebration, beautifully styled.

Semai Events & Decor is an Ottawa-based event decor and styling business dedicated to creating beautiful, thoughtfully designed celebrations. From intimate birthday celebrations and bridal showers to baby showers, engagement parties, weddings, and special events, we help bring your vision to life through elegant decor, creative styling, and carefully selected details.

We believe every celebration should feel personal and special. Whether you have a clear vision or need help bringing your ideas together, we work closely with you to create a setup that reflects your style, occasion, and personality.
